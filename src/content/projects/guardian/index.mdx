## Problem
Autonomous UAV operations frequently suffer from unexpected battery voltage drops and sensor degradation. Without an active supervisor, a drone may fail to execute return-to-home (RTH) before critical power depletion.

## Approach
GUARDIAN attaches as a companion supervisor via MAVLink to ArduPilot SITL. It continuously computes estimated energy-to-home taking dynamic wind vectors into account.
