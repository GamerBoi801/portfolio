## Problem
Reverse engineering stripped binaries is tedious and time-consuming. Commercial decompilers often produce convoluted gotos and unreadable variable names.

## Approach
Combined tokenized assembly graphs from Ghidra with a fine-tuned transformer model conditioned on control flow graph (CFG) representations.
